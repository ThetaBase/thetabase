Why development instances are unlimited

Because they genuinely nearly are free for us. A project nobody is connected to has its process stopped, and its state is a fold over a log we already store — so waking it costs a cold start and nothing else.

We are not being generous. We are charging for the thing that actually costs money, which is a database that has to be awake at three in the morning whether or not anybody needs it.

Branching freely is the habit this product depends on you forming. Charging for it would have been working against ourselves.

Questions we would rather answer here

What happens if my card fails?

We tell you, and you keep your data. Non-payment leads to a suspended account, never a deleted one — losing a database over an expired card is not a thing we will do.

Can I get my data out?

theta eject exports to a standard form and runs without our involvement. It works on the free plan and it works if we are having a bad day.

Is there an SLA?

Not yet, and we are not going to invent one. We publish an availability figure when we have measured a production quarter, and not before.

Do I have to talk to sales?

No. There is no sales team to talk to. theta plan upgrade pro.
